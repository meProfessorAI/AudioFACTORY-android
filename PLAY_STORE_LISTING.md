## 2. Full Description

```text
Audio Factory is your professional AI voice and audio production studio on Android. Built for creators, sound designers, podcasters, and developers, Audio Factory brings studio-grade voice generation and multi-speaker dialogue synthesis directly to your mobile workstation.

KEY FEATURES:

• MULTI-SPEAKER STUDIO: Create dialogues, podcasts, and dramatic scenes with custom voice assignments, pacing controls, and emotional inflections.

• INSTANT VOICE CLONING: Capture voice samples directly through your microphone or upload audio files to synthesize custom vocal profiles in seconds.

• EXTENSIVE VOICE LIBRARY: Access natural, expressive voices across diverse tones, accents, and languages.

• STUDIO-GRADE PLAYBACK & MONITORING: Preview your tracks with ultra-low latency playback powered by modern Web Audio technology.

• LOSSLESS AUDIO EXPORTS: Export your finished projects directly to your device storage in pristine WAV, MP3, and FLAC formats.

• SEAMLESS WORKSPACE SYNC: Work flexibly with cloud project persistence that stays in sync across your desktop and mobile environments.

PRIVACY & PERMISSIONS:
Audio Factory values your creative privacy.
• Microphone: Used solely when you choose to record a voice sample for voice cloning.
• Storage: Audio downloads are saved directly to your device's public Downloads directory using modern Scoped Storage without broad storage access.

Create. Direct. Synthesize. Welcome to Audio Factory.
```
